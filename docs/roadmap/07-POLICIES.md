# 07. Business policies the system depends on

These are decisions for Operations, Finance and Legal, not for engineers. The software enforces them, but it cannot invent them. Each needs an owner's sign-off before the feature that relies on it goes live in the pilot.

Engineering rules (R-*) live in [RULES-AND-POLICIES.md](../architecture/RULES-AND-POLICIES.md). These P-* items sit above them.

| ID | Policy | Owner | Needed by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| P-DG-1 | **Write-off approval.** Approval limits by value: a depot operations manager approves up to LKR 25,000; the Head of Distribution approves above that, and any Tech line. The person who decided the delivery may not approve its write-off. Values are entered by hand, because the catalogue has no prices. **Finance confirms the thresholds before the pilot** | Finance, Head of Distribution | FF-03 |
| P-DG-2 | **Photos before release.** The loader photographs every Tech line and any damaged line before the vehicle is released. These photos are the "left the dock intact" record in a dispute | Operations | FF-02 |
| P-DG-3 | **Legal hold.** Retention (photos P-14, voice P-33) never deletes evidence attached to an open issue, dispute or claim. The hold lifts when the matter closes | Legal, Operations | M-01, FF-03 |
| P-DG-4 | **Who runs inquiries.** Damaged-goods inquiries belong to Operations, through the `ops_inquiry` role, not to the system administrator | Head of Distribution | FF-03 |
| P-FP-1 | **Lost or changed Fuel Pass.** If a vehicle's QR is lost, changed or re-registered: the administrator revokes the old one, uploads the new one, and the driver's phone re-syncs. Fuelling any vehicle other than the one assigned that day is forbidden | Fleet manager | FF-06b |
| P-FP-2 | **Paper fallback.** A laminated copy of each vehicle's QR stays in that vehicle, for a 04:00 reassignment with no signal or a dead phone. The app is a convenience and the fuel log, not the only way to fuel | Fleet manager | FF-06b |
| P-PD-1 | **Personal data.** Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act No. 9 of 2022 is in force from 1 January 2027, with the 2025 amendment on cross-border transfer. Before any cloud AI, speech-to-text or object storage outside Sri Lanka holds or processes staff or store data: <ul><li>staff are told in writing what is collected (GPS while on a run, voice notes, photos), and give consent where required;</li><li>staff can ask for their profile photo to be removed;</li><li>a data processing agreement and a cross-border transfer note exist for each vendor;</li><li>recordings used to test speech-to-text are collected with consent.</li></ul> | Legal | FF-05, FF-07, M-01, Phase 5 |
| P-AI-1 | **AI controls.** <ul><li>No message to a store or anyone outside the company without a person approving it.</li><li>A monthly spend cap with alerts, and a kill switch.</li><li>Every prompt and answer is recorded.</li><li>No personal data or voice to a foreign model without a data processing agreement.</li><li>Each AI feature must show a KPI gain in shadow before it goes live.</li><li>No model can lower an emergency.</li></ul> | Business owner, Engineering lead | Phase 5 |
| P-MSG-T1 | **Message template wording.** Operations owns the wording of dispatcher reply templates, and the Head of Distribution approves it. A native speaker signs off each language. Templates are versioned, and each sent message records which version it used. New wording ships in the weekly release, never as a live edit during a shift | Head of Distribution | FF-08 |
| P-FUEL-Q | **Fuel quota source.** The data gives each vehicle 340-620 L a week; the National Fuel Pass allows 200 L per lorry and 50 L per van. Finance confirms whether the fleet has a commercial allocation, and which figure planning must use. Quotas are configuration with an effective date, never code, because the national rules change often | Finance, Fleet manager | Planning, FF-06 |
